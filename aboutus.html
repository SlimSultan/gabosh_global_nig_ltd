Who We Are

Company Overview

Established in 2012, Gabosh Global Nigeria Limited (GGNL) is a leading construction and real estate company with a strong presence in Abuja and Kwara State. In addition to its operational base in these regions, the company is strategically positioned to expand its footprint across Nigeria.

GGNL builds for comfort, quality, and long-term value. Through a combination of on-site professional management and office-based expertise in engineering, design, and project planning, supported by modern tools and systems, the company consistently delivers projects that meet high industry standards.

The company specializes in a broad range of services, including general building contracting, real estate agency services, property consultancy, project supervision, building materials supply, and interior and exterior design. GGNL is equipped to handle diverse construction and development projects, providing tailored solutions that align with client needs and project specifications.

To ensure efficient project execution and logistical support, GGNL leverages reliable access to construction equipment, materials, and supply chains, enabling seamless operations regardless of project scale or location.

Driven by innovation and a commitment to excellence, GGNL continuously adopts modern construction techniques and best practices to improve efficiency, durability, and overall project outcomes. This forward-thinking approach has positioned the company as a trusted partner in Nigeria's construction and real estate sector.

With a clear focus on quality delivery, client satisfaction, and strategic growth, Gabosh Global Nigeria Limited remains committed to strengthening its presence in the industry while exploring opportunities for expansion both locally and internationally.
